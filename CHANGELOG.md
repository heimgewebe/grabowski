- Added Operator Context Fabric V1, a compact read-only composition surface for exactly three profiles (`pr`, `bureau`, `deployment`). It performs no I/O, keeps no memory database, no chat history and no write-back path: callers pass observations that existing authorities already produced, and a fixed source registry binds each of them to the authority that owns that truth and refuses claim types the declared authority may not establish. Every claim carries `claim_type`, authority, scope and binding, `observed_at` or an explicit historical marker, evidence refs, sensitivity, status and `as_of`-derived freshness, conflicts and `does_not_establish`. Composition fails closed on an incomplete target binding, on a missing required authority and on a claim budget too small to carry the required authorities; contradictions are preserved and linked rather than resolved; evidence references use closed key and type allowlists with validated repository and digest fields, HTTPS-only URLs and no local paths, payloads carrying secret-bearing keys are rejected outright, and no source may exceed the internal sensitivity ceiling. Contexts are sealed with a canonical digest, which `grabowski_context_fabric_explain` and `grabowski_context_fabric_compare` verify before interpreting or diffing them; comparison is limited to one identical target binding and asserts neither progress, regression nor approval.
